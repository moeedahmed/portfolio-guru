## 1. Screening, scope and ownership

**Assessment outcome: OPEN; residual risks have not been accepted.** Do not reuse the earlier DPIA's assertions of confirmed large-scale processing, executed DPAs or low residual consent/logging risk. The repository does not establish those facts.

Purpose: assist a clinician to capture clinical learning through Telegram, select and review portfolio drafts, save approved drafts/evidence to Kaizen, and review portfolio progress. Account management, optional style learning, billing, monitoring and recovery are ancillary activities.

This merits DPIA assessment because it combines potentially identifiable health information, AI, third-party messaging, credentials/session storage and multiple recipients. Whether the precise processing is legally high risk, “large scale”, or requires a DPO is **OPEN O1–O2**, informed by real volumes, subject vulnerability, geography, frequency and data categories. Do not decide scale from a beta label or company size alone. ## 3. Necessity, proportionality and alternatives

These are assessments for review, not declarations that every existing implementation choice is necessary.

| Choice | Purpose and less intrusive alternative | Decision still required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hosted drafting | Reduces manual portfolio work. Alternatives include clinician-written structured fields, local-only parsing/transcription and submitting less detail. | O2/O5: document why each AI-bound field/media item is needed; consider local redaction before hosted media processing and removal of unnecessary fallbacks. |
| Long-lived password storage | Supports unattended re-authentication. Passwordless connection is already an alternative; a short-lived session still grants account access. | O6: make informed choice possible, minimise session duration/scope and confirm platform terms. Do not call password storage universally necessary. |
| Case reuse and recovery | Avoids lost work and supports another form from the same case. | O8: justify the last-filed exception, bound memory/disk copies and distinguish working-case, retry backup and disaster-recovery windows. |
| Style learning / Health | Personalises writing and identifies evidence gaps. | O7: separate optional choice, prefer structured features over raw examples/free text, review contamination and retention. |
| Cloud mirror and backup | Availability and disaster recovery. | O7/O11: justify each copied field and duplicate store; mirror excludes filing narrative but is not the whole backup system. Test restore without resurrecting erased data. |
| Logging and consent evidence | Reliability, security and accountability. | O9/O10: restrict free text, set finite schedules and minimise retained identity. “Append-only forever” is implementation, not a necessity assessment. |

**Legal basis remains OPEN O2.** Candidate contract, legitimate-interest or legal-obligation bases must be attached to particular activities and subjects; do not stack bases indiscriminately. A clinician cannot supply another person's explicit consent by accepting a bot notice. Determine the status of genuinely anonymous versus re-identifiable data and the separate confidentiality/institutional duties. For personal health information, assess the applicable Article 9 condition alongside Article 6 and any relevant DPA 2018 requirements. [ICO special-category guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/special-category-data/what-are-the-rules-on-special-category-data/) supports this two-part analysis; it does not approve this product's chosen label.

## 5. Rights, erasure and consent evidence

Reset requires confirmation, attempts local deletion and appends withdrawal; normal reset retains consent and billing rows and does not erase Kaizen entries ([backend/bot.py:9925](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/backend/bot.py#L9925), [backend/bot.py:9990](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/backend/bot.py#L9990), [backend/usage.py:141](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/backend/usage.py#L141)). Mirror deletion returns failures/skips that the caller does not turn into a complete erasure receipt ([backend/supabase_sync.py:296](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/backend/supabase_sync.py#L296)). This is not proof of full rights fulfilment. The assessor session/notification/state stores are not enumerated by this reset helper (O13).

O8–O9 must provide a verified contact route, identity check, subject-access/correction/export/restriction workflow, retention exceptions and complaint handling. Withdrawal recording errors are caught by reset; prove failure handling as well as the successful case. Do not promise immediate complete erasure, an unimplemented `/export`, subscription cancellation or deletion from independent recipient systems.

Any replacement consent copy remains **DRAFT FOR REVIEW only** and must disclose the final evidenced recipient/retention position. Legal/clinical approval, a new version/archive and adequate re-notification must precede any later change; this task makes none.
