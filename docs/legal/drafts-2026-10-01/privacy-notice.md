## 3. Reasons and legal bases

**OPEN O2 — solicitor decision, not settled wording:** the final notice must identify the legal basis for each purpose and subject. Proposed candidates are contract for the service you request, assessed legitimate interests for necessary security/reliability, and identified legal obligations for specified compliance/billing records. These are not yet approved bases for every row above.

Where health information identifies someone, an applicable special-category condition is also needed. Your agreement to use Portfolio Guru does not constitute a patient's consent to processing their health information. Removing obvious identifiers does not necessarily make information anonymous. [ICO guidance on special-category data](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/special-category-data/what-are-the-rules-on-special-category-data/) describes the separate Article 6 and Article 9 requirements.

The current implementation records a clinician's consent version, text hash, choice, channel and timestamp. It checks for the current version before case processing and records withdrawal on confirmed reset. This describes the software, not a conclusion that the chosen legal basis is valid ([backend/consent.py:49](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/backend/consent.py#L49), [backend/consent.py:71](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/backend/consent.py#L71), [backend/consent.py:111](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/backend/consent.py#L111)).

## 5. Storage and security

The application uses local SQLite databases, JSON state, conversation persistence and temporary files on its service machine. It applies Fernet encryption to selected credential fields, saved browser state and dedicated working/draft files. **This is not whole-database encryption or proof that every stored copy is encrypted.** Account/profile/consent metadata, ordinary pickle state, attachment caches and local backup archives have different protections. ([backend/credentials.py:95](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/backend/credentials.py#L95), [backend/clinical_persistence.py:123](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/backend/clinical_persistence.py#L123), [backend/clinical_persistence.py:215](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/backend/clinical_persistence.py#L215), [scripts/backup_db.sh:127](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/scripts/backup_db.sh#L127).)

Passwordless connection avoids deliberately saving the entered password in the broker's session record; your credentials still pass through that broker, and the retained session itself permits account access. Choosing passwordless requests removal of a previously stored password, including a best-effort mirror deletion. Remote deletion can fail ([backend/kaizen_connection.py:75](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/backend/kaizen_connection.py#L75), [backend/credentials.py:148](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/backend/credentials.py#L148), [backend/supabase_sync.py:82](https://github.com/moeedahmed/portfolio-guru/blob/a0301fd5f87badb103738ea54abeef31bb6297e3/backend/supabase_sync.py#L82)).

**OPEN O3/O6/O10/O11:** verify host location, disk encryption, authorised access, key management, broker transport, logs, all persistence paths and recovery copies. Do not claim that no password is ever handled, no clinical data enters logs, or all personal data is encrypted at rest.
